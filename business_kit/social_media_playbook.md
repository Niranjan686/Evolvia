## 🎥 Platform 2: YouTube & Loom Video Funnel (The Closer)
Whenever you build any cool feature (the CCTV attendance, the AI phone agent, the CRM):
1. Record a **2 to 3 minute Loom video**:
   - 0:00 - 0:30: Show the problem (e.g. "Schools losing track of bunking students").
   - 0:30 - 2:00: Show the working system live on your screen.
   - 2:00 - 2:30: Call to Action: *"If you want this set up for your institution, link in description to book a 15-minute architecture call."*
2. Post this on **LinkedIn, Twitter (X), and YouTube**.
