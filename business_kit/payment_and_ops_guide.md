# Global Payment Processing & Banking Setup Guide

How to bill and collect payments from clients in the **United States, UK, Europe, UAE, and Worldwide** directly into your Indian bank account with minimal transaction fees.

---

## 1. Top Recommended Payment Gateways for Global Clients

### Option A: Wise Business (Formerly TransferWise) — *Lowest Fees (Under 1%)*
- **Why it's best**: Gives you local US (Routing & Account Number), UK (Sort Code), and European (IBAN) bank account details.
- **How it works**: US clients pay you via domestic ACH transfer or wire (costs them $0). Wise automatically converts USD $\rightarrow$ INR and deposits directly into your Indian bank within 24 hours.

### Option B: Stripe (Invoice & Credit Card Payments)
- **Why it's best**: Allows international clients to pay with their credit card via a single click on an invoice.
- **Setup in India**: Sign up for **Stripe India**. Provide your PAN card and GST (if applicable). Stripe handles automatic currency conversion into INR.

### Option C: Razorpay International
- Activate the "International Payments" toggle in your Razorpay dashboard. Supports USD, EUR, GBP, and AED.

---

## 2. Invoicing Best Practices
1. **Always invoice in USD ($)** for US and global clients. A price like "$1,500" feels normal to an American business owner, whereas quoting in INR creates confusion.
2. **Payment Milestone Standard**:
   - Never start a custom project without a **50% upfront deposit**.
   - The remaining 50% is paid upon delivery before handing over production admin keys.
